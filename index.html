# Privacy Policy **Pocket Fishing Village** **Last updated:** October 11, 2026 Pocket Fishing Village is developed by Pixivo. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. ## 1. Information Collection Pocket Fishing Village does not require account registration or personal information to play. Game progress, coins, fish collections, upgrades, and preferences are stored locally on your device. ## 2. Use of Information Locally stored game data is used only to save your progress, remember your settings, and provide gameplay features. The Game does not send this saved information to us. ## 3. Third-Party Services The current version does not include third-party advertisements, analytics, or tracking services. We do not sell or share players' personal information with advertisers. ## 4. Data Storage and Security Your game progress is stored locally on your device. We do not operate a remote database containing player accounts or game saves. Data remains on your device until removed through the Game's reset option, app storage settings, or uninstalling, subject to device backup settings. ## 5. Data Deletion You can delete your saved game progress using the reset option in the Game's settings or by clearing the application's storage on your device. ## 6. Children's Privacy The Game does not require players to provide personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Game. ## 7. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy if the Game's features or data practices change. Updates will be reflected in the latest published policy. ## 8.
