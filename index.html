Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Pixivo ("we", "us", or "our") develops and provides Pocket Fishing Village (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play the Game.

1. Information We Collect

The current version of Pocket Fishing Village does not require an account and is designed to be played offline. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location to play.

The Game stores gameplay information locally on your device, including fish collections, coins, upgrades, achievements, settings, and saved progress. This information is used to save and restore your game and is not sent to us by the Game.

2. How Information Is Used

Locally stored information is used to provide game features, remember preferences, and maintain your progress between sessions. No personal information is needed to play.

3. Advertising, Analytics, and Sharing

The current version of the Game does not integrate third-party advertising networks or analytics services, and it does not sell or share players' personal information with advertisers.

4. Storage, Security, and Retention

Saved game data is stored on your device using local application storage. We do not operate a remote player-profile database for this Game. Local game data generally remains until you reset your progress, clear the Game's storage, or uninstall the app (depending on your device and backup settings). Please use your device's security features to help protect locally stored information.

5. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You may reset your saved progress through the Game's settings. You may also clear the app's storage through your device settings or uninstall the Game. Since we do not receive or store your local game saves on our servers, we cannot access or delete those local files remotely.

6. Children's Privacy

The Game does not require players to provide personal information. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the Game. If you have a privacy concern involving a child, please contact us.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Game changes. We will publish the updated text and change the "Last updated" date. If we introduce advertising, analytics, accounts, online services, or other features that change how data is handled, this policy will be revised accordingly.
